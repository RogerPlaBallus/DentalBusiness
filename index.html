FAQ

Common questions

Do you accept Catalan public health coverage? +

We work privately and with most private insurers. Reimbursement paperwork is provided for each treatment.

How long is a first appointment? +

About 45 minutes: interview, scan, X-ray and a written plan with prices.

Are treatments painful? +

Local anaesthesia with gentle technique, plus sedation option for anxious patients.

Can I pay in instalments? +

Yes — financing up to 24 months at 0% APR for treatments over €500.

Is there a guarantee? +

12 months on fillings, crowns and implants; 2 years on aligners.
